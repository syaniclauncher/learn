## microsoft_auth
These notebooks contain examples of Microsoft authorization for a Minecraft Launcher in a simplified way so anyone can implement them by learning from these in their own launcher :)

The step 1 & 2 are different in these two notebooks, rest of the steps (step 3 and after) are basically the same.

> [!WARNING]
> **NEVER use the OAuth Client IDs provided in the examples**
>
>  They are for demonstration & educational purposes only and belong to their respective launchers. Always use your own OAuth Client ID when building your own Minecraft Launcher.
